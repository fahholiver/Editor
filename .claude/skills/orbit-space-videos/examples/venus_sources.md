# Sources for "What Would Kill You First On Venus"
- Surface temperature ~464 °C, pressure ~92× Earth (~93 bar, like ~1 km under the ocean), sulfuric acid clouds ~45–70 km, 96.5% CO2, cloud-top winds ~300 km/h circling the planet in ~4 days, 243-day retrograde rotation vs 224.7-day orbit, conditions at ~50 km close to Earth's surface pressure, 2023 Magellan reanalysis showing a vent changing over 8 months: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venus (citing NASA/ESA sources)
- Venera 13: landed 1 March 1982, designed for ~32 min, survived at least 127 min, ~457 °C and ~89 atm measured: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venera_13
- NASA HAVOC airship concept (2015): https://www.wikiwand.com/en/Colonization_of_Venus
- Images: NASA/JPL Magellan PIA00271 (global radar view) and PIA00106 (Maat Mons, vertical scale exaggerated 10×). NASA media guidelines: https://nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media
