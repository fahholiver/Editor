# Legal and trust rules (read before research)

These protect the user's channel. Follow them even if the user asks otherwise, and explain why in one line.

**Numbers and facts**
- Every number on screen has a source and a year in `sources.md`. Charts use only real data points; never fill a missing year by guessing (leave it out).
- Estimates are said as estimates ("about", "roughly"). Different years are never mixed in one claim.
- No invented quotes. A quote card needs who said it and where it was published. Paraphrases are labelled as someone's account ("as Netflix co-founder Marc Randolph remembers it").
- No investment advice and no predictions about stocks.
- Living people: stick to the public record (their decisions, filings, court rulings, published interviews). Say "accused" until a court decides. No claims about anyone's private life.

**Images and sound**
- Photos only from free licences through `scripts/photos.py` (public domain, CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA), credited on screen and in the description. US federal government photos are public domain.
- Company logos appear only as they really look in real photos (a store sign); never redraw a logo or use one as a graphic.
- Crop out private bystanders' faces; never show a private person as if they were part of the story.
- Never use clips or stills from TV news, films, other YouTubers or paid stock sites.
- Music and effects are generated by `engine/audio.py` (no claims). A track in `music/` must allow YouTube use (see `music.md`).

**Titles and thumbnails**
- True to the video. Numbers in the title and thumbnail must match the sources.
