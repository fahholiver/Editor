# Free music and sound effects for YouTube

The engine composes its own score (no claims ever). Use these when the user wants a real track or extra sounds. Always check the licence on the download page; licences change.

| Source | What | Credit needed | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| YouTube Audio Library (YouTube Studio → Audio Library) | Music + sound effects | Some tracks (filter "Attribution not required") | Safest for monetised videos. Search moods "Dramatic", "Dark", "Calm"; genre "Cinematic". |
| Pixabay Music / Sound Effects | Music + effects | No | Huge. Some tracks are registered with Content ID; a claim is usually cleared with the licence page. |
| Incompetech (Kevin MacLeod) | Classic cinematic, epic, ancient | Yes (CC BY) | Put the credit line from the site in the description. |
| Free Music Archive | Ambient, orchestral | Depends on the track | Only CC BY or CC0. Never NC (NonCommercial). |
| Mixkit | Music + effects | No | Good whooshes, impacts, ambiences. |
| Uppbeat (free plan) | Polished cinematic | Yes | Free plan limits downloads per month. |
| Freesound.org | Effects (crowds, swords, horses, fire, wind) | CC0 no; CC BY yes | Filter by licence. |
| ZapSplat | Effects | Yes (free plan) | |
| Sonniss GDC bundles | Pro effects packs | No | Free yearly packs, royalty-free forever. |

Avoid: BBC Sound Effects (not for monetised use), anything NonCommercial (NC) or NoDerivatives (ND) for edited videos, commercial songs, music ripped from films, games or other channels.

How to use a track: save one MP3 into the project's `music/` folder. The engine loops it with a crossfade, levels it to the score's loudness, keeps it softer under chapter cards and ducks it under the voice. Add any required credit line to `upload.md`.
