September 23rd, 2010.

A company with thousands of stores files for bankruptcy.

Ten years earlier, it could have stopped all of it. For fifty million dollars.

In the year 2000, the founders of a tiny website flew to Dallas with an offer. Their company was losing money. It mailed DVDs in red envelopes.

For fifty million dollars, Blockbuster could own it.

Blockbuster's CEO was trying not to laugh. The answer was no.

That website was Netflix.

At its peak, Blockbuster had more than nine thousand stores and made around six billion dollars a year. In 2000 alone, it collected eight hundred million dollars in late fees.

Today, there is one Blockbuster left on Earth.
