This company could have owned Netflix for fifty million dollars.

In 2000, Netflix was a tiny website losing money. It mailed DVDs in red envelopes. Its founders flew to Dallas and offered the whole thing to Blockbuster.

Fifty million dollars. Blockbuster made that much from late fees in about three weeks.

Blockbuster's CEO was trying not to laugh. The answer was no.

Ten years later, Blockbuster was bankrupt. Today, there is one Blockbuster left on Earth.

All because of a fifty million dollar no.
