# Bundled fonts

The engine embeds two open fonts so videos look the same on macOS, Windows, WSL and Linux:

- **Anton** (titles and big text), by Vernon Adams. SIL Open Font License 1.1.
- **Patrick Hand** (handwritten notes), by Patrick Wagesreiter. SIL Open Font License 1.1.

Both come from Google Fonts via Fontsource. The OFL allows free use, embedding and redistribution, including in commercial videos. https://openfontlicense.org
