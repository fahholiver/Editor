# Format: confession — "I" narrator, one strange night told in first person (the mall-mannequins style)

Follow the 5-step formula in `STORY_BRIEF.md`. Model: *"I got locked in the mall overnight. The mannequins move
when the lights go out. And the lights go out when you stop moving."* It is fiction: do not claim it really
happened, no real names, places or dates.

## Shape
1. **Hook = where I am trapped, already in danger (line 1)** — the situation, not the backstory
   (*"I got locked in the mall overnight."*, *"I still don't know who I let into my flat that night."*).
2. **The two linked rules (lines 2–3)** — a mechanic that chains into itself so every action has a cost
   (*X happens when Y; Y happens when I do Z*). The viewer now watches every move I make.
3. **Mid-action, then three escalating beats**, each a concrete visible object, sound or count (a hand on
   a railing, fingers peeled off "one… one…", the lights clicking off), each breaking or bending the rules.
4. **Outside voice that contradicts what I see** — a call or text from someone safe (*"Security has you on
   camera 12. There's nothing on that floor with you."*). Who is right?
5. **Sting = a count or detail that includes me** (*"41. 42. 42 was wearing a red hoodie just like mine."*).
   End on it, then a one-word comment question that echoes the hook (*"Would you have stopped moving? Yes or no."*).

## Voice
First person, past tense, plain words, short lines. Characters: the narrator (Daniel or Emma rig), plus at
most one other figure. Settings from the engine (flat, hallway, kitchen, bathroom, lift, bedroom). Keep the set the engine can draw — no crowds
(*41 mannequins* is said and counted, shown as silhouettes or a number tag, not animated as a crowd).

## Title / caption
Title = what happened to me (*Locked In Overnight*, *I Let Him In*).
