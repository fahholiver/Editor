# Format: confession — "I" narrator, one strange night told in first person

A first-person account of one night, told like someone confessing it calmly. It is fiction: do not claim
it really happened, no real names, places or dates.

## Shape
1. **Hook = the worst detail first** (one line): *“I still don't know who I let into my flat that night.”*
2. **Setup** (2–3 lines): where I was, the time, one ordinary routine.
3. **Three small wrong things**, each a little worse, each a concrete visible object or sound (the door
   chain already on, two toothbrushes wet, the dog growling at the sofa).
4. **The turn** — I understand what the wrong things mean, in one plain sentence.
5. **Sting** — what I found/heard last, closer than before. End on it.

## Voice
First person, past tense, plain words, short lines. Characters: the narrator (drawn with the Daniel or
Emma rig), plus at most one other figure. Settings from the engine (flat, hallway, kitchen, bathroom,
lift, bedroom).

## Title / caption
Title = what I did (*I Let Him In*, *I Answered the Door*).
